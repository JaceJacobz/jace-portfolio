About

Three lanes, one person.

Software gives the ideas structure. Music gives them feeling. Film gives them a frame. I move between all three, on purpose.

I'm a software developer based in Delta State, building lightweight AI tools and clean web products. Off the clock, I'm writing Afrobeats records that fuse Yoruba rhythm with modern production, and directing short films that chase visual stories most people scroll past.

I don't treat these as separate careers — the same instinct that debugs a race condition also finds the right cut point in a scene, or the pocket in a beat. If you're building something that needs a technical mind with a creative eye, that's exactly where I work best.
