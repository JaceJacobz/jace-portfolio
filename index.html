01 About

One creative mind. Three disciplines.

I work across technology, music, and visuals — three disciplines that let me approach ideas from different angles.

I'm Jacob Oghenetekevwe Idjereh, professionally known as Jace Jacobz. As a developer, I build web experiences and AI-powered tools with a focus on making technology practical and accessible. As an artist, I create Afrobeats records and develop my sound independently. Through lyric videos, I give my songs a visual side.

I'm interested in the space where technology and creativity overlap — where code can become a product, an idea can become a song, and a song can become a visual.
